# ThumbsUp! v11 5x12 Keyboard for Kailh Choc and MX-compatible switches dirven by ProMicro and pin-compatibles

Multilevel 58 Key Ergonomic Contoured Mechanical Keyboard.

A variant of v5 and v8 for this specific combination of switches, plus with extra pins for MCU to make ProGlide Cirque touchpad work in ZMK.
Added slots on the PCBs to cut off the top row and outer columns, to turn the board into a 4x12 or 5x10 or 4x10, moved the tracks out of the way for those cuts.

The board uses Kailh Choc and MX-compatible switches with hot-swap sockets.

ProMicro or pin-compatible MCUs are supported, including Raspberry RP2040, nice!nano v2 and clones.

Supports Proglide Cirque touchpad (up to 40mm diameter).

Can be made wireless with nice!nano v2 (or a clone) and a battery, [ZMK firmware.](https://github.com/ak66666/zmk-config/tree/master)
